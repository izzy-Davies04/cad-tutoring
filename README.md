# Online CAD & EGD Tutoring

A simple one-page website for my online CAD and Engineering Graphics and Design (EGD) tutoring.

I teach **Autodesk Fusion, Autodesk Inventor, Onshape and FreeCAD** in live one-on-one online lessons, for Grade 10 to 12 learners, beginners and hobbyists.

## What's on the page

- The software I teach and who the lessons are for
- How lessons work
- Prices (free trial lesson, single lesson, pack of 5)
- About me and a short FAQ
- A WhatsApp button to book a lesson
- A link to my Superprof profile

## How to edit the page

1. Open `index.html` on GitHub and click the pencil icon.
2. Find the line that starts with `var CONFIG=`. Change these values:
   - `whatsapp`: your number in international format, without `+` (for example `27XXXXXXXXX`)
   - `superprof`: the link to your Superprof profile
   - `trial`, `single`, `pack`: your prices
3. Click **Commit changes**. The live page updates after a minute or two.

## How to put it online (GitHub Pages)

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Pages**.
2. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
3. Wait a minute or two. Your link will appear at the top of the Pages screen, usually `https://yourusername.github.io/repository-name/`.
