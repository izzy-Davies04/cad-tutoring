Questions

Do I need to buy software?

Not necessarily. Onshape and FreeCAD are free, and Autodesk offers free options for students and personal use. Check the current terms for your situation.

What do I need for lessons?

A laptop or PC that can run your chosen software, a stable internet connection, and Zoom or Google Meet.

Can you help with my PAT or school project?

Yes. I’ll teach you the skills and guide you, but the work you hand in should be your own.
